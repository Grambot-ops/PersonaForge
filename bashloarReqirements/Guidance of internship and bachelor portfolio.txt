Internship guidelines
4
(Internship) supervisor = TM-colleague
• Point of contact during the internship
• Meetings at school / online sessions for international internships (see later)
• Visit internship company (see later)
• Supervises the process of the internship
• Not substantive, this is done by the work placement mentor
• Reviews the internship documents (to put on the graduation portfolio)
(Work placement) mentor = Internship company employee
• See internship contract page 2
Internship guidelines
• Internship company/work placement mentor
• Gives internship assignment
• Takes care of
• suitable workplace
• necessary equipment (only in consultation with the
student deviations from this rule can be made, e.g.
for the use of the laptop)
• sufficient professional support
• Evaluates
• Via internship portal
• If the work placement mentor on your contract is not
known or is incorrect, no one can evaluate you...

Internship guidelines
6
Internship period: Full-time for 13 weeks according to the work schedule of the internship company
If 1 course in 2nd year: 1 fixed half-day per week at school and 1 week extra as compensation
Presence
Present every working day according to the schedule of the internship company.
Exceptions:
- Meetings (at school)
- Closing days are discussed with student and internship supervisor
Internship guidelines
7
Absence
• Due to illness (unexpected):
• Inform work placement mentor as soon as possible (by phone!)
• Inform internship supervisor also (e-mail)
• Send doctor's certificate to work placement mentor and internship
supervisor
• Other reasons (planned)
• discuss BEFOREhand with work placement mentor and internship
supervisor and ask permission
• e.g. Job fair on February 25th (13h – 17h): work placement mentors are
informed and kindly asked to allow you to go to the job fair
• If no permission, you are not allowed to be absent
• Valid absence should not be catched up

10
Search for internship

Initiation phase
11
Introduction to workplace and mentor
Deepening the internship assignment:
• Project Plan for internship assignment (see later)
Create a status report (Example of weekly internship status report on Canvas)
• Update daily with
• Work performed
• Problems encountered (and solutions)
• Send weekly to internship supervisor and work placement mentor
• How? Mail / chat / shared drive... to be defined by the internship supervisor
during guidance meeting
Initiation phase
12
Kick-off meeting at the internship site (domestic internships only) / online
• 2nd/3rd internship week
• Persons present: student, internship supervisor, work placement mentor
• Student organises this (online) meeting:
• Arranges
• Sends invites
• Presents the project plan of the internship to the internship supervisor and
the work placement mentor
• Makes meeting minutes
• Internship supervisor chairs the meeting
Realization phase
13
Execution of internship assignment (keep status report up-to-date...)
Problems on/with internship?
• 1st contact work placement mentor (content)
• 2nd contact internship supervisor (process supervision)
Internship Assessment / Evaluation
• Based on the competences (see Canvas document)
• Intermediate (after 4-6 weeks):
• work placement mentor discusses with student and fills in on the internship portal
• = "Formative" (<-> summative at the end of the internship) = not final
• Important learning moment
• Final (at the end)
• = Summative: qualitative assessment by the work placement mentor is translated
into a quantitative score by the jury
Realization phase
14
Meeting 1 On campus / Online session:
• Internship week 4: Internship supervisor and "his" internship students
• Each student gives a presentation of the project plan (+/-10 minutes) to the
fellow students and the internship supervisor
• The project plan to present = the project plan presented during the kick-off
meeting but taking into account the remarks that were made at that time.
• The internship supervisor gives (again) feedback. The internship student
makes notes in order to make improvements later (see feedback moment 2).
Realization phase
15
Meeting 2 On campus / Online session
• Student sends final presentation of “project plan" to internship supervisor at
least 1 week in advance
• Student gives a presentation of about 10 minutes:
• the realisations until then
• work to be carried out = updated planning
• First version of the realization document with full table of contents
• Student receives feedback (and takes note)
Realization phase
16
Working on the “Required documents of internship" (see also later in this
presentation)
Review by internship supervisor
• = SERVICE!
• Submit week 12 (at the latest) to the internship supervisor for review. After this
date, the student may still make changes but without review by the internship
supervisor.

Project Plan of the Internship Assignment
21
• Company & Background
• Brief introduction to the internship company and the context of your assignment.
• Objectives
• Clearly describe what should be achieved by the end of the internship.
• Business Case
• Explain the added value of the project and how it supports the company’s goals.
• Planning
• Overview of the project timeline and key milestones.
• Progress Reporting
• How you will keep your workplace mentor and internship supervisor informed
(frequency and communication format).
• Which project management tool will be used to track progress.
• Team & Responsibilities (optional)
• Include only if the project setup requires clarification beyond the previous sections.
