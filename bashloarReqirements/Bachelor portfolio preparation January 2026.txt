Jury Presentation

Duration
 Minimum 15min - maximum 20min
Content
 Start with a great introduction (first impression)
 Use a visually appealing slidedeck to support your story
 Select the topics you want to talk about
 Remember: you’re applying for the title of ‘Professional Bachelor’ in IT
 Jury members have reviewed your portfolio, so don’t just repeat / re-use
 Internship is the main topic, but summarize (limited time)
 Select a couple of other achievements / projects as well, so the jury can

 Remember: if it’s a team project, highlight your own contributions
 Hardest part: choose wisely, you have limited time!
Jury Presentation
22
Practical tips
 Take precautions to be on time!
 Be there at least 15min before your scheduled timeslot
 Internship supervisor will briefly introduce the jury members before the
start of your presentation
 Timing is crucial (min 15min – max 20min)
 Don’t drag it out!
 Try to insert ‘buffer moments’: topics that you can cover swiftly or take a little
more time, depending on your current timing
 Like a demo, or a technical overview image: highlight only the crucial items, or go a
little more in depth
 Like a reflection slide, where you add a couple of icons
 Don’t use bullet points, otherwise you have to cover them all
 Icons are ‘vague’ enough, so you can cover them swiftly, or lengthly
 Do a trial run!
Jury Presentation
23
Practical tips
 Dress code
 Be professional: smart – business casual
 Answering questions
 You are probably pretty knowledgeable about the topics, because you’ve spent a
lot of time on them
 But: jury members might ask questions from their own perspective
 If it’s a little out of scope, or you don’t exactly know:
 Try to answer to the best of your knowledge, from your own perspective
 If you have no clue beware of the pitfall of fabricating answers
 Don’t dig your own grave.
 After the presentation and the Q&A
 You’ll be asked to leave the room for a moment, so the jury can discuss
 You’ll be called back in to receive some feedback on how you did
